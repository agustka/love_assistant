# BetterHalf voice — one-page cheatsheet

Quick reference for fast review. For the full reasoning, see `SKILL.md`. For grep-style banned-word lists, see `banned-phrases.md`.

---

## The pitch in one sentence

*BetterHalf is a thoughtful friend who knows your partner combined with a secretary who handles the artifacts. Not AI, not an Assistant, not a reminder app. Built solo by Ágúst Karlsson.*

## The 7 bright lines

1. **No AI branding.** Never "AI", "Assistant", "Smart", "Intelligent", "Magic", etc.
2. **First-person founder voice.** "I built", never "we built." Solo builder.
3. **Gender-neutral default.** "They/their" for the partner. Gendered copy only in targeted ad campaigns.
4. **No fancy punctuation.** No em-dashes (—), curly quotes (" "), curly apostrophes (' '), middle dots (·), or decorative arrows (→). Use ASCII only.
5. **No marketing fog.** No "revolutionize", "unlock", "transform", "mental load", "be more thoughtful", etc.
6. **No "so I built this" trope.** Trust the reader to connect the problem to the product.
7. **Truthful first-person.** Don't invent Ágúst's failures. Use only verified ones (overwhelm → procrastinate → realize too late).

## The mental model

| Half | Role | Copy signals |
|---|---|---|
| Friend | Taste, judgment, knowing your partner | Low-number options, "for who they actually are", specific personalization |
| Secretary | Drafts, picks, plans, handles artifacts | "Drafts the message", "Lines up gifts", "Plans the night", timing ("ready before") |

**The user always picks.** "You pick, you send" — pre-empts the "is this faking my feelings?" objection.

## Five-second review

Read the line aloud. If it sounds like:
- A brand → rewrite
- A real person → ship

Useful gut check: *Would Ágúst send this exact sentence to a friend in a text?* If no, it's not in voice.

## Patterns by context (quick)

- **Buttons:** verb-first, concrete. *"Send draft"* not *"Submit"*.
- **Errors:** honest, gentle, actionable. *"That email doesn't look right. Try again."*
- **Empty states:** explain why empty, what to do next. No emoji.
- **Success:** matter-of-fact. *"Saved."* not *"Awesome! 🎉"*.
- **Push:** specific reason, no clickbait, respect attention.
- **Confirmations:** state the consequence directly. *"Delete this draft? You can't recover it."*
- **Loading:** honest about mechanism. Never *"Magic is happening..."*.

## Strip these words on sight

| Softeners | Brand voice |
|---|---|
| just, simply, easily | Hey there!, friend!, Yay! |
| actually (filler) | Awesome!, Perfect!, Great! |
| only (minimizing) | Oops!, Whoops!, Uh oh! |
| Don't worry | We're sorry, We apologize |
| Please / Kindly | We've got you covered |

## Allowed deviations

- **Targeted ad campaigns** can use gendered copy where the wedge calls for it.
- **Customer-voice content** (a drafted message *from* the user *to* their partner) follows the natural pronouns of that content, not the founder voice.
- **Strong stylistic exclamation** in rare deliberate cases (almost never — default to periods).
